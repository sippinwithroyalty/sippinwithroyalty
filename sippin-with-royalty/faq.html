Frequently Asked Questions ❓

How early should I book?

Please book at least 2 weeks before your event.

What events do you do?

Birthday parties, festivals, and private events.

How much does it cost?

Birthday parties and private events:
Host Pays: $75 for 40 drinks.
Guests Pay: $50 booking fee. Guests buy drinks for $3, $4, or $5.

Festivals:
No booking fee. Guests buy drinks for $3, $4, or $5.

Can I pick my drinks?

Yes! Pick from our regular menu or ask for a custom drink.

Do you travel to events?

Yes! We travel in Washington County and nearby areas.
